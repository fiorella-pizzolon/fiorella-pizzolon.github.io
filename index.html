Fiorella Pizzolon

Assistant Professor of Economics · Ph.D., University of Virginia

I am an Assistant Professor of Economics at Hamilton College. I obtained my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Virginia.

My research interests are macroeconomics, monetary and fiscal policy and international economics. Recent work studies how governments in emerging economies choose the currency denomination of their sovereign debt, and how monetary policy reaches households differently depending on where they sit in the wealth distribution.

Single-authored working paper

Why did Brazil, Colombia, Mexico and Peru replace foreign-currency debt with local-currency debt, while Uruguay turned instead to inflation-indexed debt? A small open economy model where a government without commitment chooses inflation and the issuance of three kinds of bonds.
