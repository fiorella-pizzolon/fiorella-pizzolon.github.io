Fiorella Pizzolon

Assistant Professor of Economics, Hamilton College

I work on macroeconomics, monetary and fiscal policy, and international economics. My recent research studies how emerging economies choose the currency denomination of their sovereign debt, and how monetary policy reaches households differently across the wealth distribution. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from the University of Virginia.
